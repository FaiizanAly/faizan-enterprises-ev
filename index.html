About Us

A Family-Run Electric Mobility Business

Faizan Enterprises has been a trusted name in electric mobility in Senthal, Bareilly since 2017. We are a local, family-run dealership dedicated to making clean, affordable electric transportation accessible to our community.

We offer a carefully selected range of electric scooters and E-Rickshaws to suit the practical needs of everyday riders and commercial operators alike. Beyond the sale, we provide ongoing service, repairs, and spare parts — because our commitment doesn’t end at delivery.

Muzaffar Ali leads the business and customer-facing operations day-to-day. Faizan Ali manages our digital presence, finance documentation, online payments, delivery photography, and social media — bringing a modern approach to a neighbourhood dealership.
